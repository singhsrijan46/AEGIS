Feasibility, 3-Member Allocation & Stepwise Plan

Demonstrating technical feasibility, 3-member role allocation, and 5-step implementation roadmap

1. 3-MEMBER WORK DIVISION

• Member 1 (AI/NLP Lead): E-AFV atomic claim extraction, LangGraph orchestration, Hybrid KG-RAG, Minimal Perturbation rewriter.

• Member 2 (Vision & Security): OpenCV colorimetry (ΔE < 2.0), WCAG 2.1 contrast, logo safe-zones, air-gapped VPC & PII scrubbing.

• Member 3 (Full-Stack & Product): Next.js web portal, FastAPI gateway, Docs/Figma plugins, TipTap in-line diffs, FTC/SEC rule mapping.

• Compute: 8B quantized model on 1x 16GB GPU (120 pgs/min throughput).

2. POTENTIAL CHALLENGES & RISKS
  • #1 Data Leakage: Pre-release copy leaking to public cloud LLMs.
  • #2 Voice Flattening: Aggressive rewriting destroying author tone.
  • #3 Stale Facts: Publishing expired pricing or deprecated SLAs.
  • #4 Conservative Bias: Over-flagging benign metaphors as violations.
3. MITIGATION STRATEGIES
  • Air-Gapped Private VPC: Zero Data Retention (ZDR) + local vLLM.
  • Minimal Perturbation (MPED): Token masking freezes author voice.
  • Temporal Knowledge Graph: Facts tagged with strict expiry dates.
  • Calibration Prompts: MT-bench swap-evaluation stops false flags.
