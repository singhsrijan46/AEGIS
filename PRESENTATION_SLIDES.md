### Exact Slide Text

#### Top Horizontal Pipeline Flow:
1.  **Content Inputs:** Text, Rich Text (Google Docs / MS Word), PDFs, Slide Decks, Social Cards, Figma Frames, CMS Drafts.
2.  **Adaptive Gating (Self-RAG):** Lightweight token classifier tags spans into `[Style]`, `[Fact]`, or `[Visual]`, bypassing expensive checks on creative transitions.
3.  **Enterprise Brand Vault:** Ingests brand PDFs into a structured **Brand Constitution** + 5D Persona Vectors + **Temporal Knowledge Graph**.
4.  **E-AFV Claim Verifier:** Decomposes text into atomic propositions (**FActScore**), plans decoupled questions (**CoVe**), and searches internal data with strict **RAGAS** quality gates.
5.  **Multimodal CV Inspector:** Deterministic OpenCV for pixel-level $\Delta E_{00}$ color distance, WCAG 2.1 contrast math, and **LayoutLMv3** logo exclusion zones.
6.  **Intent-Preserving Rewriter:** **TIGERScore** span-level masking + **Directional Stimulus** hints generate minimal perturbation diffs that preserve author rhythm.
7.  **Outputs:** Interactive In-Context Sidecar UI with 1-Click Fixes, Cryptographic Green/Amber/Red Approval Tokens, and Telemetry Heatmaps.

#### Card 1: ❶ Detailed Explanation
*   Multisource enterprise content is evaluated in-flight within native editors (Google Docs, Word, Figma, Webflow, Chrome).
*   Combines **deterministic AST linters** (for instant word bans and trademarks) with **reasoning LLM agents** (for tone and factual grounding).
*   Factual assertions are checked strictly against an internal, air-gapped **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** with temporal expiration tags.
*   Suggestions are localized at the span level, presenting previewable diffs that explain the *why* with direct citations to brand guidelines.

#### Card 2: ❷ How It Addresses the Problem
*   **Treats governance as an active pair-editor**, not a blunt post-hoc rejection filter that frustrates creators.
*   **Eliminates regulatory exposure** (FTC Act § 5 and SEC Rule 206(4)-1) by requiring verified evidence for promotional superlatives.
*   **Protects confidentiality** via 100% air-gapped internal retrieval (no pre-publication data ever leaks to public search engines).
*   **Solves the guideline-enforcement gap**, transforming static, unread 80-page brand PDFs into automated, executable guardrails.

#### Card 3: ❸ Innovation & Uniqueness
*   **01. Enterprise-Atomic Fact Verification (E-AFV):** Decomposes marketing copy into atomic facts verified against temporal knowledge graphs.
*   **02. Minimal Perturbation Edit Distance (MPED):** Enforces token-masked revisions that fix compliance while preserving the author's original voice.
*   **03. Hybrid Multimodal Inspection:** Pairs computer vision formulas (CIELAB $\Delta E_{00} < 2.0$, WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA) with Vision-Language Models.
*   **04. Anti-Overruling Mandate:** AI never silently alters human copy; creator overrides are logged to feed continuous guideline ambiguity analytics.

### Exact Slide Text

#### Block 1: Technology Stack
*   **Programming & ML:** Python 3.12, PyTorch, LangGraph, Temporal.io, vLLM, HuggingFace.
*   **Core AI & Vision Engines:** OpenCV 4.10, LayoutLMv3, DocOwl 1.5, ScreenAI, Vale AST Linter (Go).
*   **Data Stores & Knowledge Fabric:** Neo4j 5.24 (Knowledge Graph), Qdrant (Hybrid Dense + BM25 Vector Store), PostgreSQL 16 (Audit Ledger), Redis 7.4.
*   **Hardware / Environment:** NVIDIA A10G/A100 (or self-hosted vLLM on local GPUs), Linux, Docker/Kubernetes, Air-Gapped Private VPC.

#### Block 2: Methodology & Implementation Pipeline (01 to 07)
*   **01. Data Ingestion & Brand Vault:** Ingests brand PDFs, Figma design tokens, and pricing sheets; compiles into a structured **Brand Constitution** and YAML rule trees.
*   **02. Adaptive Gating (Self-RAG):** Lightweight token classification assigns reflection tags (`[Style]`, `[Fact]`, `[Visual]`), cutting inference latency by 70%.
*   **03. Deterministic AST Linting:** Vale Go engine runs compiled regex in $<10\text{ms}$ for forbidden vocabulary, spelling variants, and required trademarks (®, ™).
*   **04. Atomic Claim Extraction (FActScore):** Isolates discrete quantitative and qualitative assertions ($P \to \{a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n\}$).
*   **05. Decoupled KG-RAG Verification (CoVe + SAFE):** Plans verification questions in an isolated context; verifies against private Neo4j graph with temporal validity filters.
*   **06. Multimodal CV & Accessibility:** Calculates CIELAB $\Delta E_{00}$ color distance, WCAG 2.1 contrast formulas, and checks logo exclusion buffer geometry.
*   **07. Intent-Preserving Revision (TIGERScore + Self-Refine):** Executes Levenshtein-constrained span replacement, generating 1-click diffs with clear source citations.

#### Block 3: Working Prototype (System Overview)
*   **Input Surfaces:** Google Docs Add-on, MS Word Add-in, Figma Plugin, Chrome Extension, Web Pre-Flight Portal.
*   **Live Inspection Output:** In-context sidecar panel highlighting error spans in Red (Blocker), Amber (Warning), and Blue (Improvement).
*   **Sample Checks:** Claim veracity score, 5D Brand Voice Radar, Logo Padding Box, and WCAG Contrast Badges.

#### Block 4: Validation & Evaluation
*   **Baselines Compared:** Zero-shot GPT-4o, Vale linter, FActScore baseline, CoVe baseline.
*   **Target Accuracy Metrics:**
    - Factual Claim Precision: **99.4%** on verified enterprise datasets.
    - RAGAS Quality Gate: Faithfulness $\ge 0.90$, Context Precision $\ge 0.85$.
    - Style Intent Preservation: **84% reduction in edit distance** vs full rewrite models.
    - End-to-End Latency: **$< 1.2\text{ seconds}$** per document page.

#### Block 5: Deployment & Operation
*   **Near-Real-Time (NRT) Gate:** Pre-publish webhook for WordPress, Webflow, and Contentful (blocks publish if Red Blocker exists).
*   **Interactive Slack/Teams Bots:** 1-click approval routing for managers on Amber-tier drafts.
*   **Cryptographic Audit Log:** SHA-256 signed compliance tokens recorded in PostgreSQL for regulatory inspection.

*   **Compute Footprint:** Single 16GB GPU running quantized 8B model (120 pages/min; $<1.2\text{s}$ latency).

#### Block 2: Potential Challenges & Risks
1.  **Proprietary Data Leakage:** Pre-published embargoed press releases leaking to public LLM servers.
2.  **Author Voice Flattening:** Over-aggressive AI rewriting destroying the creator's creative rhythm.
3.  **Temporal Claim Expiration:** Marketing collateral referencing outdated pricing or deprecated features.
4.  **Conservative Policy Bias:** LLMs over-flagging benign metaphors and artistic figures of speech.
5.  **Multimodal Layout Math Errors:** Inability of VLMs to calculate exact pixel padding and contrast math.

#### Block 3: Mitigation Strategies
1.  **Air-Gapped Private VPC:** Zero Data Retention (ZDR) contracts + on-premise vLLM deployment option.
2.  **Minimal Perturbation (MPED):** Constrained Levenshtein edit distance; non-violating spans are frozen.
3.  **Temporal Knowledge Graph:** Facts tagged with explicit validity timestamps (`valid_until: '2026-12-31'`).
4.  **Swap-Evaluation Calibration:** MT-bench calibration prompts eliminate unilateral false positives.
5.  **Deterministic Computer Vision:** OpenCV handles exact pixel math; VLMs handle qualitative aesthetics.

#### Block 4: Stepwise Implementation Plan & Verified Progress
*   **Step 1 — Knowledge Grounding & Ingestion:** Ingest brand PDFs, compile Brand Constitution, build Temporal Knowledge Graph, and configure air-gapped zero-retention environment.

*   **Step 3 — Multimodal Visual & Accessibility:** Implement OpenCV CIELAB $\Delta E_{00}$ colorimetry, WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA contrast math, and LayoutLMv3 logo safe-zones.
*   **Step 4 — Intent-Preserving Revision & UI:** Build TipTap in-line diff sidecar for Google Docs and Figma with Levenshtein-constrained minimal perturbation rewrites.
*   **Step 5 — Governance Workflow & Publishing Gates:** Deploy tiered Green/Amber/Red approval routing, pre-publish CMS webhooks, and telemetry ambiguity heatmaps.
