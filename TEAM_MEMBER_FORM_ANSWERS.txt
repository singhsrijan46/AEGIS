Explain your Solution in 4-5 lines *

[QUESTION 4]
How does it work? Explain your technical approach. *

ANSWER:
Our architecture combines deterministic rules with agentic multi-hop reasoning across 5 validated steps:
1. Brand Ingestion: Ingests brand PDFs into a machine-readable Brand Constitution and a Neo4j Temporal Knowledge Graph tagging facts with validity timestamps.
2. Atomic Verification: Decomposes complex assertions into atomic propositions (E-AFV) and generates decoupled verification queries (Chain-of-Verification) to eliminate confirmation bias.
3. Reflection & Quality Gating: Employs Self-RAG reflection tokens and RAGAS faithfulness thresholds (>= 0.85) to score confidence before approval.
4. Minimal Perturbation Rewriting: Uses Levenshtein-constrained token masking to fix flagged spans while keeping surrounding author voice intact (cosine >= 0.94).
5. Publishing Gates: Triggers pre-publish CMS webhooks and Slack approval bots based on Green/Amber/Red risk triage.

[QUESTION 4]
How does it work? Explain your technical approach. *

ANSWER:
We implement a multi-stage deterministic and agentic pipeline:
1. Air-Gapped Privacy Boundary: Incoming drafts pass through Microsoft Presidio for PII scrubbing before entering an on-premise/private VPC execution environment.
2. Multimodal Document Layout Parsing: LayoutLMv3 and DocOwl 1.5 extract visual guidelines, logo buffers, and color palettes from brand manuals.
3. Deterministic Pixel Math: OpenCV calculates CIELAB Delta-E 00 color distance against approved swatches (Delta-E < 2.0) and verifies WCAG 2.1 relative luminance contrast ratios (4.5:1 for body, 3.0:1 for headers).
4. Spatial Layout Auditing: Validates logo safe-zone buffer boundaries (S >= 0.5 * H_logo) and aspect ratio distortion.
5. Cryptographic Compliance: Every audited asset generates an immutable SHA-256 compliance token recorded in PostgreSQL for regulatory defensibility.

[QUESTION 4]
How does it work? Explain your technical approach. *

ANSWER:
The system operates as an in-context sidecar directly connected to our microservices gateway:
1. In-Flight Capture: Browser extensions and native workspace add-ons stream document ASTs over secure WebSockets to the FastAPI gateway.
2. Real-Time Evaluation: Dispatches text and assets across parallel verification workers (lexical linters, factual checkers, and CV engines).
3. Non-Destructive In-Line Diffing: A TipTap-based sidecar renders green insertions and red strikethrough deletions alongside rule citations and confidence scores.
4. Automated Risk Triage: Routes drafts into Green (instant publish), Amber (peer review), or Red (legal escalation) queues.
5. Pre-Publish Webhook Gates: Intercepts publish actions in CMS platforms (WordPress/Webflow), requiring an approved compliance token before assets go live.
